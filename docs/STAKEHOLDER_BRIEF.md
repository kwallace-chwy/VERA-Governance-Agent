# Stakeholder brief

## What VERA is

VERA is a governed agentic AI workflow for employee voice review. It helps HR Compliance, Employee Relations, and Labor Relations understand what Team Members are raising through VOC Board feedback and where sites may need additional support.

## The business problem

VOC Board feedback gives leaders a valuable view into the Team Member experience, but open-ended comments are hard to review consistently at scale. Sensitive topics may be rare, indirect, or mixed into broader operational feedback.

The current process relies on keyword filtering and manual review. That process is important, but it can be hard to measure and hard to explain across review cycles.

## The value

VERA helps the business move from one-time searching to a repeatable review system.

It gives teams:

- Faster visibility into themes that may need human attention.
- Clearer routing to HR Compliance, ER, LR, Safety/EHS, Legal, or site HR.
- More consistent documentation of why a comment was surfaced.
- Better trend visibility across sites, months, and business units.
- A way to monitor follow-through and support positive employee relations.

## Employment-law lens

VERA should be framed as an early visibility and routing tool. It should not be framed as determining whether harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or protected activity occurred.

The most important legal guardrails:

- Human review remains required.
- Outputs should avoid legal conclusions.
- Labor relations/protected activity must be routed carefully.
- Raw comments should be restricted.
- Review decisions and follow-through should be documented.

## Compliance lens

VERA creates a more objective method for review by:

- Defining review domains.
- Applying consistent logic across the comment population.
- Preserving reason codes.
- Measuring false positives and false negatives.
- Creating an auditable gold standard.

## HR VP lens

VERA helps leaders see where support may be needed sooner. The business value is not just finding sensitive words. The value is helping teams understand patterns, route support, monitor follow-through, and keep the employee experience at the center of the work.

## Employee Relations lens

VERA helps ER distinguish isolated comments from repeated themes. It can support coaching, site support, trend monitoring, and follow-through visibility without turning every comment into a formal case.

## Labor Relations lens

VERA separates labor relations/protected activity signals from general compliance and ER categories. That distinction helps ensure the right partners review those comments with appropriate context.

## Recommended positioning

Use:

"VERA is a governed agentic AI workflow for employee voice review. It helps teams see emerging themes sooner, route the right partners, and support stronger follow-through."

Avoid:

- "VERA identifies harassment."
- "VERA finds discrimination."
- "VERA determines actionable reports."
- "VERA replaces HR review."

## Current baseline from July 9, 2026 run

The July 9, 2026 provided Snowflake export contained:

- 6,085 VOC Board comments.
- 423 VERA review candidates.
- 69 Priority 1 candidates.
- 238 Priority 2 candidates.
- 116 Priority 3 candidates.

These are prevalence and routing signals, not legal findings.
